Having a child changes your financial and legal situation significantly. Getting a few things sorted before the birth makes the transition easier.

## Paid parental leave

You must have worked for your current employer for an average of 10 hours per week for at least 6 months before the due date. Primary carers are entitled to up to 26 weeks paid leave. Partners are entitled to 2 weeks. Payment is at your regular rate up to a weekly maximum — check employment.govt.nz for current figures.

Your job is protected during paid parental leave. You can extend to 52 weeks total (26 unpaid) with job protection throughout.

## Before going on leave

Notify your employer at least 3 months in advance if taking more than 4 weeks. Apply for the parental leave payment through Inland Revenue before you go on leave. Check whether your employer tops up the government payment.

## Best Start payment

$73 per week for each child for the first year, regardless of income. After the first year, continues until age 3 subject to income thresholds. Apply through Inland Revenue.

## The financial reality

Initial gear: $2,000 to $5,000. Nappies and consumables: $1,500 to $2,500 per year. Childcare after parental leave: $250 to $450 per week for under-2s in most regions. The 20 Hours ECE subsidy applies from age 3.

## Legal steps before baby arrives

Update your will — particularly to appoint a guardian. Update your KiwiSaver nomination of beneficiary. Review your insurance — life insurance and income protection become significantly more important when a child depends on your income.
