## How much do you actually need?

At 20% of the purchase price: a $700,000 house needs a $140,000 deposit, a $1,000,000 house needs $200,000.

## Use your KiwiSaver

If you have contributed for at least 3 years, you can withdraw most of your balance for a first home purchase (leaving $1,000 in the account). This is often the single biggest source of deposit funds for first home buyers. Apply through your KiwiSaver provider — start the process at least 10 days before you need the funds.

## The First Home Grant

Up to $5,000 per person for an existing home, or $10,000 per person for a new build. For a couple buying a new build, that is $20,000 of grant money. Subject to income caps and house price caps. Check kaingaora.govt.nz for current eligibility criteria.

## The First Home Loan

Allows certain buyers to access lending with just 5% deposit. Subject to income caps. Check with participating lenders.

## What actually accelerates saving

**Automate it** — treat your deposit savings like a non-negotiable bill. Transfer on the day your pay arrives into a dedicated high-interest savings account.

**Reduce fixed costs** — rent is the biggest lever. Moving to a cheaper area or living with family can dramatically accelerate the timeline.

**Track your progress visibly** — knowing you have $47,000 of a $140,000 goal is more motivating than an abstract savings directive.

**High-interest savings accounts and term deposits** — compare rates at interest.co.nz. Rates vary significantly between banks.
