## Do you need to file?

Most people who earn only from salaried employment do not need to file a tax return. Inland Revenue automatically calculates whether you paid too much or too little and sends you the difference.

You do need to file an IR3 if you have income from self-employment, rental income, overseas income, or earn over $200 from interest and dividends.

## The myIR account

Go to ird.govt.nz to create your myIR account. You will need your IRD number. Log in after April each year to check your automatic income tax assessment. If you only have PAYE income, review it, confirm it is correct, and any refund is paid into your account within a few days.

## The tax year

1 April to 31 March. Returns for that year are due by 7 July.

## Common reasons for a refund

- You had more than one job and the secondary employer deducted at the wrong rate
- Your income was irregular
- You worked only part of the year

## Tax codes

When you start a job, complete a tax code declaration (IR330). The standard code for a main job is M. Secondary jobs use S or SH. Getting this wrong is one of the most common reasons for an unexpected bill.

## Student loans

If you have a student loan, repayments are deducted automatically at 12 cents in the dollar on income above the threshold. Make sure your tax code includes SL.

## Getting help

IR helpline: 0800 775 247. Filing your own return through myIR is straightforward for most people.
