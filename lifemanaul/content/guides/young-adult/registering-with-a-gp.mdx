## Finding and registering with a GP

Search at healthpoint.co.nz for GPs in your area accepting new patients. Look for one that is close to where you live, has online booking, and charges fees you can manage.

To enrol, contact the practice and ask. You will need your NZ Health number and ID.

## What things cost

GP consultation fees vary — typically $20 to $50 for enrolled patients. If you are on a low income, a Community Services Card reduces costs significantly. Apply at Work and Income.

## ACC

ACC covers treatment for injuries caused by accidents — regardless of fault, income, or insurance. This includes doctor visits, physiotherapy, surgery, and loss of earnings. It does not cover illness. When you see a doctor for an injury, tell them it was an accident and they will register the claim.

## When to go where

**Call 111** — life-threatening emergencies. Chest pain, stroke symptoms, serious injuries, difficulty breathing.

**Emergency Department** — serious but not immediately life-threatening. Deep cuts, suspected fractures, head injuries. Note: ED waits are often several hours for non-critical cases.

**Urgent care clinic** — non-life-threatening conditions that cannot wait for a GP. Usually faster and cheaper than the ED.

**GP** — ongoing health concerns, referrals, mental health support, routine checks.

**Healthline** — 0800 611 116. Free 24/7 nurse advice line. Call if unsure whether something needs urgent attention.

## Getting the most out of a GP appointment

Know what you want to cover before you go. If you have multiple concerns, mention them all at the start. Be specific about symptoms — when they started, how severe, what makes them better or worse. Ask questions if you do not understand something.
