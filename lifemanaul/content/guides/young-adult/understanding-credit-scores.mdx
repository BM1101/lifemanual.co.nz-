Your credit score matters when you need a loan, a rental property, or a phone plan on credit. Building a good one from the start puts you in a stronger position for major financial decisions later.

## What is a credit score?

A number calculated by credit bureaus summarising how reliably you repay debts. In NZ, scores typically range from 0 to 1000. Above 700 is good. Above 800 is excellent.

## What goes into your score

**Payment history** — the most important factor. Even one missed payment can significantly affect your score.

**Credit utilisation** — how much of your available credit you are using. Under 30% is good.

**Length of credit history** — older accounts help your score.

**New credit applications** — every application leaves a hard inquiry. Multiple applications in a short period lower your score.

**Defaults** — unpaid debts passed to a collection agency significantly damage your score and remain for up to 5 years.

## Checking your credit report

You are entitled to a free report from each bureau annually.
- Equifax NZ: equifax.co.nz
- Centrix: centrix.co.nz

Check for errors — mistakes in credit reports are more common than you would expect. If you find one, contact the bureau to dispute it.

## Building good credit from scratch

**Pay everything on time** — the most important thing you can do. Set up automatic payments.

**Get a credit card and use it carefully** — spend a small amount monthly and pay the balance in full every month. This builds credit history without paying interest.

**Do not apply for lots of credit at once** — space applications out.

**Keep accounts open** — older accounts help your credit history length.
