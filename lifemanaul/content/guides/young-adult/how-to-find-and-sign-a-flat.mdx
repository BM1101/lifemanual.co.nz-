Flatting for the first time is exciting. The single most important thing you can do is understand what you are signing — and what rights you have once you do.

## Finding a flat

The main platforms are Trade Me Property (trademe.co.nz/property) and Homes.co.nz. Facebook groups also have listings, particularly for flatmates.

When viewing, test hot water and appliances, check for dampness or mould, check heating, and ask why the current tenant is leaving.

## The tenancy agreement

Read every clause before signing. Key things to check:

**Type** — periodic (ongoing) or fixed-term (specific period). Fixed-term is harder to exit early.

**Bond** — by law, must be lodged with Tenancy Services. You should receive a receipt. Maximum 4 weeks rent.

**Rules** — pet and smoking prohibitions must be in the agreement to be enforceable.

## Your rights as a tenant

**Notice before entry** — your landlord must give at least 24 hours notice before entering, except in genuine emergencies.

**Repairs** — your landlord is responsible for keeping the property in reasonable repair. Notify them in writing and keep a record.

**Healthy homes standards** — NZ rentals must meet minimum standards for heating, insulation, and ventilation.

**Rent increases** — only once every 12 months with 60 days notice.

## The bond

Document everything on move-in — take timestamped photos of every room before unpacking. This evidence is invaluable if there is a bond dispute when you leave.

## Getting help

- **Tenancy Services**: tenancy.govt.nz or 0800 836 262
- **Tenancy Tribunal**: resolves disputes for free
- **Citizens Advice Bureau**: free general legal advice
