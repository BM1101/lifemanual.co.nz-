Most young workers do not know their basic employment rights — which means they sometimes get less than they are entitled to.

## Your employment agreement

Every employee in NZ must have a written employment agreement before they start work. This is a legal requirement. Read it before you sign. You are entitled to take it away to consider.

## Minimum wage

The NZ adult minimum wage (18 and over) is reviewed every 1 April. Check employment.govt.nz for current rates. Your employment agreement cannot pay you less than minimum wage regardless of what it says.

## Rest and meal breaks

- 2 to 4 hours: one 10-minute paid rest break
- 4 to 6 hours: one 10-minute paid rest break
- 6 to 8 hours: one 10-minute paid rest break and one 30-minute unpaid meal break
- Over 8 hours: two 10-minute paid rest breaks and one 30-minute unpaid meal break

## Leave entitlements

**Annual leave** — 4 weeks paid per year, accruing from day one.

**Sick leave** — 10 days paid per year from the start of employment.

**Public holidays** — if you work on a public holiday, you are entitled to time-and-a-half plus an alternative holiday.

## Trial periods

Employers with 19 or fewer employees can offer a 90-day trial period. Must be agreed in writing before you start work. Your other rights still apply during a trial period.

## If something feels wrong

Contact Employment New Zealand — free advice at employment.govt.nz or 0800 209 020. You cannot be fired for raising legitimate concerns about your employment conditions.
