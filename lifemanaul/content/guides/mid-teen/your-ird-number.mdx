An IRD number (Inland Revenue Department number) is your unique tax identifier in New Zealand. Every person who earns money in NZ needs one. Without it, employers must deduct tax at the highest possible rate — meaning you would pay more tax than you should.

## What is it used for?

- Employment — every employer needs it to process your pay correctly
- KiwiSaver — your contributions are tracked against your IRD number
- Bank accounts — some accounts require it
- Filing tax returns

You only ever have one IRD number, and it stays with you for life.

## How to apply

Go to ird.govt.nz — the process takes about 10 minutes.

**What you will need:**
- Your full legal name
- Your date of birth
- Your current NZ address
- A valid NZ identity document — passport, birth certificate, or citizenship certificate
- Your bank account number (optional — used for refunds)

**Steps:**
1. Go to ird.govt.nz
2. Search for "Apply for an IRD number"
3. Select "New Zealand resident or citizen"
4. Fill in your personal details
5. Upload your identity document
6. Submit

You will receive your IRD number by post within about 8 to 10 working days. If you need it urgently for a job, call Inland Revenue on 0800 227 774.

## Apply before you need it

You can apply for an IRD number at any age. Applying before you start your first job means you will have it ready rather than scrambling at the last minute.

## Keeping it safe

Give your IRD number to employers when asked — this is normal and required. Do not share it unnecessarily with people or organisations who do not need it.
