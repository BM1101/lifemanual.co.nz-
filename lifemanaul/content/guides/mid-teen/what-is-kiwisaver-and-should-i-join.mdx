KiwiSaver is one of the best financial decisions available to young New Zealanders — and most teenagers do not know it exists until their first employer signs them up years later.

## What is KiwiSaver?

KiwiSaver is a voluntary savings scheme set up by the NZ government to help people save for retirement. Your contributions are invested in a fund that grows over time. You cannot access the money until you turn 65 (with limited exceptions).

**Employer contributions** — if you are employed, your employer is legally required to contribute at least 3% of your gross wages on top of your pay. Free money.

**Government contributions** — the government also contributes up to $521.43 per year if you contribute at least $1,042.86 yourself. Also free money.

**Investment growth** — your contributions grow over time. The earlier you start, the more compound growth has to work.

## Why starting young matters so much

Money invested at 16 has roughly 49 years to grow before you reach 65. Money invested at 25 has 40 years. Those early years are the most valuable because growth compounds — your returns earn returns, which earn returns.

## How to join

Ask your employer when you start work — they can enrol you. You can also enrol directly through a KiwiSaver provider at their website. You will need your IRD number.

## Choosing a fund

**Conservative** — mostly bonds and cash. Low risk, low growth. Appropriate only for people close to retirement.

**Growth** — mostly shares. Higher risk short-term, much higher growth over long periods.

**Aggressive** — almost all shares. Highest expected long-term growth.

If you are 16 to 40, you should almost certainly be in a growth or aggressive fund. You have decades before retirement — short-term market downturns do not matter, and long-term the higher-growth funds significantly outperform conservative ones.

## Contribution rates

You can contribute 3%, 4%, 6%, 8%, or 10% of your gross pay. The minimum is 3%. At 3%, your employer also contributes 3% — a total of 6% of your salary going into KiwiSaver every pay cycle.
