Getting your licence is a major milestone — and the first step is the learner licence.

## When can you apply?

You can apply for your learner licence from age 16. There is no minimum wait after turning 16.

## Learner licence conditions

- You must always have a supervisor in the front passenger seat
- The supervisor must hold a full NZ licence and have held it for at least 2 years
- You must not drive between 10pm and 5am
- You must display L plates
- Your blood alcohol limit is zero

You must hold your learner licence for at least 6 months before sitting the restricted licence test.

## Study the road code

The test is based on the NZ Road Code, available free at nzta.govt.nz. The DriveTest app (free, iOS and Android) has practice tests that mirror the actual questions. Take practice tests until you are consistently getting 34 to 35 out of 35.

## The test

Taken at an NZTA-approved testing agent — most AA branches and VTNZ centres.

- 35 multiple-choice questions
- You need at least 32 correct (91%) to pass
- On a computer at the testing centre
- Cost: approximately $43.90 (check nzta.govt.nz for current fees)

Bring photo ID and the test fee. Read each question carefully — some are deliberately worded to catch people who skim.

## Practising on your learner

The NZTA recommends at least 120 hours of practice before sitting your restricted test, including at least 10 hours of night driving. Use the 6 months well — practise in rain, at night, on motorways, and on rural roads.
