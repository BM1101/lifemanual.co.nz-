You do not need to become a good cook. You need to be able to feed yourself without spending all your money on takeaways. These 10 recipes require almost no prior experience and basic equipment.

## 1. Scrambled eggs
Whisk 2 to 3 eggs with a splash of milk and a pinch of salt. Heat a pan on medium-low, add butter. Pour in eggs. Push gently with a spatula in slow folds. Remove while still slightly underdone. Low heat and patience — high heat makes rubbery eggs.

## 2. Pasta with tomato sauce
Boil pasta in heavily salted water. While it cooks, fry minced garlic in olive oil for 1 minute, add a can of crushed tomatoes and herbs, simmer 10 minutes. Drain pasta, combine with sauce.

## 3. Stir-fry
Heat a pan or wok until very hot. Add oil, then protein. Add garlic and ginger. Add vegetables (hard ones first — carrot, broccoli — then softer ones). Add soy sauce. Serve over rice or noodles.

## 4. Fried rice
Heat oil in a hot pan. Add garlic, then vegetables. Push to the side, scramble 2 eggs. Add day-old rice (fresh is too wet), break up clumps. Add soy sauce and toss.

## 5. Roast chicken thighs
Pat dry with paper towel (important — moisture prevents crisping). Rub with oil, season generously. Place skin-side up on a roasting tray. Roast at 200 degrees for 35 to 40 minutes.

## 6. Vegetable soup
Cook onion and garlic in oil until soft. Add harder vegetables (carrot, potato), then softer ones (zucchini). Pour over stock to cover. Simmer 20 to 30 minutes until tender.

## 7. Toasted sandwich
Butter the outside of two slices of bread. Fill with cheese and any other filling, press together, toast in a pan over medium heat pressing down with a spatula. Flip when golden.

## 8. Baked potato
Prick with a fork several times (skip this and it may explode). Rub with oil and salt. Bake at 200 degrees for 45 to 60 minutes until skin is crispy.

## 9. Banana pancakes
Mash 1 ripe banana, whisk in 2 eggs until smooth. Pour small circles into a medium-heat pan with butter. Flip when edges look set.

## 10. Lentil dal
Cook onion in oil until soft. Add garlic, ginger, and curry powder. Add 1 cup red lentils, 1 can tomatoes, 400ml stock or coconut milk. Simmer 20 minutes. Season well. Serve over rice.
