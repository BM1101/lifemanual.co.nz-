This guide gives you real, factual information — not scare tactics, not moralising. The goal is that you can make genuinely informed decisions.

## Why this matters more for teenagers

The teenage brain is still developing, and it is more vulnerable to the effects of alcohol and other substances than an adult brain. The prefrontal cortex — responsible for decision-making and impulse control — is not fully developed until the mid-twenties. Regular substance use during adolescence can affect this development in lasting ways.

## Alcohol

Alcohol is a depressant that slows the central nervous system. In small amounts it reduces inhibition. In larger amounts it impairs coordination, judgment, and memory. In very large amounts it can cause loss of consciousness or death.

**Blood alcohol and the law:** The legal blood alcohol limit for driving is 0.05% for adults and 0.00% for anyone under 20.

**Alcohol poisoning signs:** confusion, vomiting, unconscious or barely conscious, blue or pale skin, slow breathing. This is a medical emergency — call 111 and put the person in the recovery position.

**NZ law:** The legal purchase age is 18.

## Cannabis

Cannabis affects perception, mood, memory, and coordination. Regular use in adolescence is associated with effects on memory and attention, particularly for heavy users. It can trigger or worsen anxiety and psychosis in people with a predisposition. It impairs driving.

## Vaping and nicotine

Nicotine is highly addictive and affects brain development in teenagers. The teenage brain becomes dependent on nicotine more quickly than the adult brain. It is illegal to sell vaping products to anyone under 18.

## Harm reduction

- Do not use substances alone
- Do not mix substances, including with alcohol
- Do not drive while impaired
- If something goes wrong, call 111 — police will generally not prosecute someone who calls for help in a medical emergency

## Getting help

- **Alcohol Drug Helpline**: 0800 787 797 — free, 24/7, confidential
- **1737**: text or call for free counselling
